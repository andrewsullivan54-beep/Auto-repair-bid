# AUTO REPAIR BIDS — Refunds, Disputes & Payout System
*(Pasted from Andrew's ChatGPT working doc, 2026-09-25)*

## PAYMENT FLOW

1. Customer reviews repair price and terms. 2. Selects BOOK & PAY. 3. Pays agreed amount through Auto Repair Bids. 4. Payment secured through marketplace. 5. Shop gets booking confirmation. 6. Shop performs authorized repair. 7. Repair marked complete. 8. Customer confirms completion. 9. Shop becomes eligible for payout. 10. Auto Repair Bids retains its fee.

## PAYOUT TIMING

Shop does NOT get the money at booking. Payment secured first, shop does the work, payout released after completion + confirmation. Exact timing TBD — could be immediate after confirmation or after a short processing period. Need a rule for when the customer doesn't respond to the completion request.

## CUSTOMER CANCELLATION

Refund determined by cancellation policy (TBD): how long for full refund, whether cancellation fees apply, what happens if parts ordered or work started, whether the platform fee is refundable.

## SHOP CANCELLATION

If shop cancels and customer didn't cause it: customer gets full refund. Possible consequences for shops that repeatedly cancel confirmed jobs (TBD).

## CUSTOMER NO-SHOW

Needs a no-show policy (TBD): full or partial refund to customer, compensation to shop for missed appointment, effect of ordered parts, whether platform keeps its fee.

## REPAIR DISPUTES

Disputed repair → payout temporarily held during review. Marketplace must keep full documentation: original request, submitted bid, accepted price, authorized scope, customer acceptance of terms, terms/policy version, date/time of acceptance, payment record, selected shop, booking info, approved add-ons, completion info. Clear transaction history for both sides.

## ADDITIONAL REPAIRS

Shop cannot auto-increase the bill. Additional repairs need separate customer approval: customer gets the request and can APPROVE ($175) or DECLINE. Shop cannot charge unless approved. Approval recorded with amount, description, date/time.

## CHARGEBACKS & PAYMENT DISPUTES

Payment provider handles: customer payments, shop payouts, refunds, payment disputes, chargebacks, connected shop accounts, business/identity verification. Auto Repair Bids keeps transaction/authorization records to document disputes.

## COMPLETION CONFIRMATION

Shop marks REPAIR COMPLETED → customer gets request: CONFIRM COMPLETION or REPORT A PROBLEM. Confirm → shop eligible for payout. Report → dispute process, payout held.

IMPORTANT RULE: customer must not be able to block legitimate payout forever by ignoring the request → need an automatic payout rule (exact time period TBD).

## CORE PRINCIPLE

BOOKING DOES NOT EQUAL SHOP PAYOUT. Flow: CUSTOMER PAYS → PAYMENT SECURED → SHOP PERFORMS AUTHORIZED REPAIR → REPAIR COMPLETED → CUSTOMER CONFIRMS OR COMPLETION PERIOD EXPIRES → DISPUTES ADDRESSED → SHOP ELIGIBLE FOR PAYOUT → PLATFORM RETAINS FEE → SHOP RECEIVES PAYOUT.
